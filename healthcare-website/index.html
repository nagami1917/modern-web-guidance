Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with all major insurers plus self-pay plans. The booking flow shows your exact cost before you confirm — no estimates, no surprises.

How fast can I be seen?

Same-day slots open every morning at 7 am. Urgent issues are triaged by our 24/7 nurse line, which can escalate you to a clinician within hours.

Can I choose my own doctor?

Yes. Browse clinician profiles, pick the one who fits, and they become your consistent point of care across visits and messages.

Is my data safe?

Your records are encrypted in transit and at rest, shared only with clinicians on your care team, and never sold. You can export or delete your data at any time.

What if I need a specialist?

Your primary care team refers you inside Meridian first — cardiology, mental health, and diagnostics are in-house — and coordinates externally when sub-specialty care is needed.
